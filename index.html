A Letter to My Classmates

Dear friends,

It feels like yesterday we walked into class as strangers, and today we are walking out as a family. Thank you for the shared tiffins, last-bench jokes, bunked periods, exam-night panic, and the endless laughter. Thank you for every time you lifted me up.

Our school days are over, but the friendships will not be. Different colleges, different cities, different dreams, but the same hearts. Keep in touch, call often, and never forget the people who knew you before you became who you will be.

Wherever life takes us, remember: we were here, together, and it was beautiful.

— Your Classmate ❤️
