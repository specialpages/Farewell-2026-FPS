01 / A NOTE TO US

Some places
stay with you.

And some people become a part of you. This is for the ordinary days that turned out to be everything.

“We didn't know we were making memories. We were just having the time of our lives.”
To the friends who made mornings brighter, classes shorter, lunch breaks louder and difficult days easier — thank you. For the shared notes, the borrowed pens, the laughter that got us in trouble, and the little moments nobody else would understand.

Tomorrow, our paths may look different. New campuses, new plans, new beginnings. But a part of us will always be sitting in that same classroom, saving a seat for each other.
With love, Class of 2026.
